About the samples

The four included scenarios were transcribed from the supplied workbook. Old stamp duty, LMI, fees and interest assumptions are preserved as historical sample inputs, not current market values.

The older $180k/$210k sheets used some simplified monthly-rent and cost assumptions, so the app keeps their inputs but recalculates results consistently with the modern model.
